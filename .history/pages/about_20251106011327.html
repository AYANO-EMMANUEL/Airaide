Who We Are

Founded in Nigeria, AIRAIDE aims to democratize access to technology in Africa. We believe innovation should be locally developed and globally competitive. Our team combines expertise with an understanding of African markets. We create solutions that drive transformation, building the infrastructure for Africa's digital future.

Our Vision
